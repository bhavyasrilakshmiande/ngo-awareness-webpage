ABOUT US

InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020. We are committed to bringing positive change in society through our key initiatives in education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental conservation and community support.

With a strong network of volunteers and partners, we strive to create a more inclusive, compassionate and empowered society.

OUR PROJECTS

Project BachpanShala

Providing education and learning support for underprivileged children.

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development and financial independence.

Project Jeev

Animal rescue, feeding and protection drives for stray animals.

Project Prakriti

Environmental sustainability through tree plantation and awareness programs.

Project Seva

Providing food and clothing support to needy communities.
