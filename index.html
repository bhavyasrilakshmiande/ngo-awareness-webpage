About The NGO

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded in 2020. The organization works for education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and community development.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project BachpanShala

Providing education and learning support for underprivileged children.

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development and financial independence.

Project Jeev

Feeding and protecting stray animals with compassion and care.

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental sustainability through tree plantation drives.

Project Seva

Food and clothing support for needy communities.

Social Impact

InAmigos Foundation has impacted thousands of lives through education support, women empowerment, animal care, environmental awareness and volunteer activities.
